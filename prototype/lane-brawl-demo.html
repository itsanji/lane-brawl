Fighting

  • Chains: Light, Light, Light, or Light, Light, Heavy. Skills cancel normals on hit.
  • Up+Heavy launches. Jump after it to juggle in the air. Down+Heavy sweeps.
  • Guard beats attacks, grab (Guard+Light) beats guard, attacks beat grabs.
  • Parry: tap Guard just before a hit lands to stagger the attacker.
  • Juggle limit 4: after 4 juggle hits the victim drops and can't be hit.

Meter and the match

  • Meter (3 bars) fills from dealing and taking hits. Ultimate costs 3. Hold a skill button for an EX version (1 bar). Guard+Heavy while being comboed is a Burst (2 bars).
  • Last-hit minions for gold. Shop in your fountain. Pick upgrades at levels 3, 6 and 9.
  • Wyrm (lower jungle, 2:00) and Titan (upper jungle, 5:00) buff your team.
  • Towers → Gate → Core. Hold S+Guard for 3 s to recall.
